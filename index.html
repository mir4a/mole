Riding a

Tuned for trains: smooth runs, gentle curves, level platforms.

  1. Open this page before you go underground

    There is no network in the tunnel. The page saves itself on the phone and then works offline.

    Saving for offline use…

  2. Turn on the sensors

    The page needs the accelerometer and the gyroscope. On iPhone the browser will ask for permission.

  3. Fix the phone in place

    The phone has to ride with the car and not move on its own. Holding it in your hand won't work: your hand moves more than the train does.

    Lean it against the window, bottom edge on the frame
    Lay it on the seat, screen up
    Press it against the end wall of the car
    Turn on the sensors to check

  4. Calibrate at a station

    While the train is standing at a station, tap Start and don't touch the phone for 3 seconds. The first departure after that tells the app which way is forward.

    At your last station, tap Finish as the train pulls in: you don't have to wait for it to stop.
